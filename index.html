Informatics Impact Summary

By separating chronic medication pickups (60% of clinic traffic) from doctor consultations and implementing asynchronous virtual queueing, clinic waiting rooms maintain manageable density, curbing airborne contagion (TB, Influenza, COVID) and preserving patient dignity.
